FAQ

What is Foodie Reppy?

It is a recipe website where users can browse different cuisines and learn about classic dishes.

Can I view recipes by category?

Yes. The Categories page lets you explore recipe collections by cuisine such as Filipino, Japanese, Italian, and more.

What information is included in each recipe?

Each recipe includes a short description, a list of ingredients, and images of the ingredients to help users understand the dish better.

Can I search for a specific recipe?

Yes. You can use the search feature on the Home page to look for recipes or dishes you want to find.

What cuisines are available on Foodie Reppy?

Foodie Reppy includes different cuisines such as Filipino, Japanese, Italian, Chinese, Mexican, Indian, and American cuisine.

Can I see images of the ingredients?

Yes. Some ingredients have images that can be viewed when interacting with the ingredient list.

How do I choose a cuisine?

Go to the Categories page and select the cuisine you want to explore. The available recipes for that category will then be displayed.

Is Foodie Reppy easy to use?

Yes. Foodie Reppy is designed with simple navigation so users can easily browse categories, select recipes, and view recipe information.
